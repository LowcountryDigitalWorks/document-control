# Clean-room and intellectual-property statement

This repository is an original, generic Lowcountry Digital Works implementation. It must not
contain, derive from, or disclose Coalfire proprietary code, confidential information, client
information, internal architecture, branding, or Coalfire-owned implementations.

Contributors must use public documentation, generally known engineering techniques, and
LDW-owned requirements and assets. Synthetic fixtures are required for public demonstrations
and tests. Do not copy customer documents, identities, workflow definitions, or metadata into
the repository.

If the provenance of a proposed contribution is uncertain, do not commit it. Record the concern
and obtain an LDW clean-room review first.
